# TAKSI — Unit Economics

Public unit-economics summary for partners, funds and exchanges.
The same figures are published on the OMNICOR network site.

## The model

- **0% commission on rides.** The passenger pays the driver directly —
  the platform never touches ride revenue, carries no payment risk and
  no commission overhead. This removes the dominant cost structure of
  incumbent ride-hailing.
- **Flat shift-access fee.** The platform monetizes via a fixed 12-hour
  driver access window — **$0.80–$16 localized per market**, anchored at
  roughly **one average local ride**. The driver earns the fee back on
  the first order of the day; incumbents still take 15–30% of every fare
  after that. The blended model price used below is **$3.30 / 12h**.
- **~99% automation** — matching, payments, compliance and support
  flows are automated, driving marginal cost per shift toward zero.

## Per-shift P&L (blended)

| Line | Value |
|---|---|
| Revenue — shift access fee | $3.30 / 12h |
| Direct costs — PSP & messaging | ≈ $0.09 |
| **Contribution margin** | **≈ 97%** |
| OMNI buyback allocation — capital, not cost | 15% |
| **Operating margin — after buyback & tax** | **≈ 80%** |

The OMNI buyback is a **capital allocation**, not an operating cost:
margin funds the buyback-and-burn, supply shrinks, and the
treasury-held ecosystem reserve accretes — deflation compounding
against itself.

## Model at target scale

Assumptions: ~30M ride-hailing drivers worldwide, ~22 paid shifts per
active driver·month, blended $3.30 shift fee, 15% of revenue allocated
to buyback.

| Global share | Drivers | Shifts / day | Revenue / yr | OMNI buyback / yr |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5% | 1.5M | 1.1M | $1.3B | $200M |
| 15% | 4.5M | 3.3M | $3.9B | $590M |
| **40% (target)** | **12M** | **8.8M** | **$10.5B** | **$1.6B** |

At 40% share, roughly **$120B of annual ride GMV** flows through the
platform directly to drivers — TAKSI deliberately never touches it.

Typical incumbent net margins run **10–15%**.
